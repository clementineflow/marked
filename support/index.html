Including your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened helps us investigate faster. Please do not send private quotes, notes, or screenshots containing personal information unless they are necessary to explain the issue.

Common questions

Where are my quotes stored?

Marked stores your quotes on your device. It does not create an account or sync your content to a Marked server. You can prepare an export from Settings before moving to a new device or deleting the app.

How do I restore Premium?

Open Marked, go to Settings, choose Premium, then select “Restore purchases.” Please make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that made the original purchase.

Why is a quote not appearing in my widget or reminder?

Archived quotes do not appear in widgets or reminders by default. Check that the quote is not archived and that Marked has permission to send notifications. Widget updates are managed by iOS, so they may not always appear immediately.

Why can’t I add another quote?

The free version can keep up to 60 active quotes. Archived quotes do not count toward this limit. You can archive an existing quote or choose Premium to keep more active quotes.

How do I delete all of my data?

In Settings, choose “Delete all data.” This permanently removes your locally saved quotes, tags, reminder settings, and export link. If there is anything you want to keep, prepare an export first.
